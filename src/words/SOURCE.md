# Word list source and licence

`answers.js` and `guesses.js` are generated by [scripts/build-word-lists.mjs](../../scripts/build-word-lists.mjs) from **SCOWL** (Spell Checker Oriented Word Lists), release 2020.12.07, <http://wordlist.aspell.net/>. They are not copied from the New York Times Wordle lists.

## How the lists are made

| List | Words | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Answers | 2,063 | Five-letter words from SCOWL sizes 10–35 (common words), English, American and British categories. Plurals, past tenses, taboo words and a short hand-picked exclusion list are removed. |
| Guesses | 11,184 | Five-letter words from SCOWL sizes 10–80, same categories, taboo words removed. Includes every answer. |

- Only lowercase `a`–`z` words are used, so proper nouns, abbreviations and words with accents or apostrophes are left out.
- Both US and UK spellings are included (for example FIBER and FIBRE).
- Taboo words come from SCOWL's own `misc/offensive.*` and `misc/profane.*` lists.

To regenerate, download and unpack the SCOWL release, then run:

```
node scripts/build-word-lists.mjs <path-to-unpacked-scowl>
```
