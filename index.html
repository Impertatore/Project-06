    How to play

    Find the hidden 5-letter word in 6 rows.

    • Type a word with your keyboard or the on-screen keys, then press Enter.
    • Only real English words are accepted. US and UK spellings both count.
    • After each guess, the tiles change colour to show how close you are.

    Colours

    • WGreen: right letter, right place.
    • IYellow: the letter is in the word, but in another place.
    • UGrey: the letter is not in the word (or not again).

    High contrast mode uses orange for green and blue for yellow.

    Hints

    You get 3 hints per game. A hint fills your next row with a word that shares at least one letter with the answer. It keeps your green letters in place and uses all your yellow letters, so you never lose what you have found, and it always finds something new: a new green, or a letter you hadn't found. It uses up that row. You can't use a hint on the last row, and none is given if only the answer would do that.

    Hard mode

    Green letters must stay in place, and yellow letters must be used, in every later guess. Hard mode can only be changed before your first guess or hint.

    Quitting

    Pressing New Game during a game, or reloading the page, counts as a loss and a quit in your statistics.
