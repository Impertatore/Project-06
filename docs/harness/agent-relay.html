The relay

What each handoff carries

Agents don't talk to each other directly. Each one leaves something on disk or on GitHub, and the next step reads it. That's what makes every step checkable.

The RESULT line

Every agent ends with one line in a fixed shape. The main chat relays it to you as is, and a future orchestrator could read it without parsing prose.

architectRESULT | spec=docs/changes/<slug>.md | pr=<url> | assumptions=<k> | open-questions=<k> | spec-6-conflicts=<k>
implementerRESULT | pr=<url> | branch=<branch> | tests=<pass>/<total> | manual-checks=<ids>
testerRESULT | mode=<pr|gap> | target=… | tests-added=<k> | proven-failing=<k>/<k> | possible-bugs=<k>
reviewerRESULT | target=PR #<n> | verdict=<A|S|B> | automation=<…> | comments=<k> | blocking=<b>
security-analystRESULT | target=… | verdict=<CLEAR|CONCERNS|HUMAN-REQUIRED> | certain=<k> | possible=<j>
any agentRESULT | stopped=<reason> — a valid outcome, not a failure
